Long bio · 300 words

Fernando Urbano was born in Ciudad de la Costa, Uruguay, in 1997. Curiosity was his engine from an early age: take apart, ask, put back together. At 15 he chose computer science and learned to program before he learned to sell, a combination that would shape his entire career. His first tech job came right out of high school; by 21 he was running his first regional leadership role, automating more than 80 processes with RPA across 12 countries.

He then moved into consulting: Delivery Manager and later Head of Engineering at Practia (Publicis Sapient) for Chile and Argentina, where he led an operation of more than 500 people. Consulting taught him to translate technology into boardroom language. In 2024 he crossed the Atlantic and joined Rocketbot, first in international expansion from Madrid and then leading agentic product. Today he is the company's Director of Innovation.

He has advised more than 200 clients across seven countries, was named among the 40 Under 40 Innovators Global in 2024, and won international innovation awards in 2016, 2017 and 2018. He is a recognized voice on RPA, hyperautomation, LLMs and business agents across LATAM and Iberia, speaking at universities, conferences and executive breakfasts. His hallmark is anti-bluff: facts over hype, real cases over promises. He talks about AI because he builds it.
